Our cause 🐾

Our kittens

Part of every purchase at El Jardín de los Ángeles helps feed, shelter and care for the kittens who live on the streets, and protect the littlest ones in their first days until they find a home.

How we care for them

From the street to a loving home

  1. 🍽️Food every day

    We prepare their food every day and make sure everyone eats, even the shyest ones.

  2. 🏠Shelter and warmth

    Little houses raised off the ground, with blankets, so they can rest protected from the cold and rain.

  3. ❄️In winter too

    When the snow arrives, we keep bringing them food and a warm place: that is when they need it most.

  4. 🍼Their first days

    Newborns get a bottle, warmth and lots of love, and we take them to the vet when they need it.

  5. 🏡A forever home

    Once they are healthy and strong, we look for families who want to adopt them.
