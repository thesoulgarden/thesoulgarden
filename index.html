Frequently asked questions

What people often ask us

Where does the holy water come from?

It comes from the Grotto of Massabielle in Lourdes, France. It is offered in 0.5 oz bottles and as an option on several rosaries and bracelets.

Can I choose colors or designs?

Yes. The bottle keychains come in silver, blue, yellow, green, purple and black, and each item shows its options on Etsy.

Do you accept returns?

Yes, on items that say so. Check the shipping and returns section of each product on Etsy.

Do you take custom orders?

Send us a message on Etsy or Instagram and we will gladly help with keepsakes for baptisms, communions or events.
